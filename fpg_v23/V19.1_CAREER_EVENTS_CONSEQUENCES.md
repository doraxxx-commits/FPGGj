# FPG V19.1 — Career Events & Consequences

V19.1 turns decisions from the Career Decision Center into persistent world consequences.

## Flow
Decision Center → immediate World Event → Media/Social interpretation → delayed consequence → new decision opportunity.

## Consequences
- transfer rejection / negotiation / personal acceptance;
- contract rejection / counteroffer / renewal;
- sponsor acceptance / rejection;
- interview acceptance / refusal;
- delayed contract pressure;
- delayed transfer pressure;
- sponsor momentum;
- media pressure consequences;
- fan protection during poor form;
- agent career conversations.

Cooldowns are persisted in WorldEngine save data to prevent event spam and preserve continuity after save/load.
