# FPG V18.1 — World Causality

V18.1 adds a persistent causality layer on top of the existing world engines.

## New behaviour
- Sport crisis has phases instead of one-off random news.
- Board pressure can escalate after 3 days of crisis.
- Long crisis changes transfer activity and youth focus after 10 days.
- Very long crisis can affect fan support and finances.
- Recovery is recognised after a crisis and gradually restores stability.
- Financial problems can force a cheaper recruitment strategy after 14 days.
- A player transfer request now has a remembered timeline:
  - 7 days: agent activity
  - 21 days: club becomes more open to offers
  - 35+ days: continued morale pressure
- Causality state is saved and restored with the world save.
- World history is written after all daily actions, including transfer-window actions.

## Design rule
The layer does not replace MatchEngine, TransferEngine, ManagerWorldEngine,
BoardEngine or LivingWorldEngine. It connects their consequences into longer
chains so the same world state can naturally create future events.
