# FPG V19.9 — Career Consequence Engine 2.0

V19.9 closes the loop between the player's actual match and the living career world.

## Flow

REAL MATCH → PERFORMANCE → RELATIONSHIPS → CAREER CONSEQUENCES → NEXT SQUAD DECISION → NEXT MATCH

## Match feedback

- excellent performance: stronger coach trust, happiness, fan support, fame and lower coach pressure
- good performance: small positive relationship changes
- poor performance: lower coach relationship, higher coach pressure and a possible role-risk event
- unused player: loss of confidence and a stronger risk of conflict with the coach
- win/loss is a secondary modifier; performance remains the main signal
- goals additionally increase fame, fan support and marketing value
- assists improve reputation

## Persistent state

`careerConsequenceV2` stores the last processed day per player, preventing duplicate application after save/load or repeated UI refreshes.

## Important architecture change

The GameEngine snapshots the player's real match immediately when the fixture is played. After the autonomous WorldEngine tick, V19.9 feeds that snapshot back into the WorldEngine. This means the following day's `PlayerCareer.updateMatchStatus()` sees the updated manager relationship and can change the player's squad status naturally.
