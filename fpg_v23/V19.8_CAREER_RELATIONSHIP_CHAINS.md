# FPG V19.8 — Career Relationship Chains

V19.8 extends V19.7 interactive relationship scenes into persistent multi-stage career story chains.

## Flow

WORLD / RELATIONSHIP ACTION → SCENE → PLAYER CHOICE → RELATIONSHIP CONSEQUENCES → FOLLOW-UP SCENE → RESOLUTION

## Chains

- Coach: role discussion → coach response → decisive role decision
- Agent: market signal → concrete proposal → pursue / wait / close
- Club: future meeting → club response → stay / conditions / exit
- Media: first statement → second media wave → clarify / double down / withdraw

Each chain has a stable chainId, stage and next due day. Stage progression is persisted in WorldEngine save data.

## Design rules

- A chain never creates more than one pending scene for the player.
- Follow-up scenes are delayed by at least two in-game days.
- Existing V19.7 cooldown prevents unrelated scenes from spamming the player.
- Chain responses feed the existing RelationshipWebEngine and WorldEvent pipeline.
- Older saves without chain fields remain readable; missing fields use safe defaults.

## Save

`relationshipEvents.chains` stores active chain state. Existing `relationshipEvents.pending` stores the scene itself, including chainId and stage.
