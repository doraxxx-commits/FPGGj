# FPG V19.5 — Relationship Consequences

Based on V19.4.

## Added
- concrete consequences for relationship levels
- coach support / role opportunity
- coach conflict after prolonged benching
- agent market push when transfer pull is high
- club protection during poor form
- fan protection against media pressure
- media amplification for high fame/media relationship
- club ultimatum when club + coach relationships are critically low
- persisted cooldown memory to prevent event spam
- save/load integration via `relationshipConsequences`

The relationship web now affects actual career state rather than being a passive dashboard.
