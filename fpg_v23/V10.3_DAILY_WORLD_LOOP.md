# FPG V10.3 — Daily World Loop

The world now has an explicit daily simulation pass:

1. player/AI recovery and form drift
2. injury progression and new injury risk
3. subtle daily training stimulus
4. squad selection
5. manager decisions
6. autonomous matches
7. MatchResult consequences for clubs and players
8. weekly finances when the calendar reaches the weekly tick
9. loans / transfer-market activity
10. living club dynamics
11. reputation, board, agents, player decisions, dressing room and negotiations
12. organic world events
13. club-strength recalculation
14. News/FPG Social can consume `lastDayEvents` and `lastDayMatchResults`

The daily training pass is intentionally weak. Major progression remains seasonal, preventing unrealistic OVR growth from simply clicking through days.

`WorldEngine.lastDaySummary` exposes match/event/goal counts for diagnostics and future UI.
