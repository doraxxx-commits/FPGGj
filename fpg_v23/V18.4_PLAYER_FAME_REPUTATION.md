# FPG V18.4 — Player Fame & Reputation

V18.4 extends V18.3 Media World. Fame is now a persistent player-world system rather than a visual-only media number.

## Flow
WORLD EVENT → MEDIA STORY → FAME / REPUTATION → FANS → MARKETABILITY → TRANSFER INTEREST

## Player profile
- `fame` — public visibility and recognition.
- `reputation` — slower social memory.
- `fanSupport` — relationship with supporters.
- `mediaPressure` — short-term attention and pressure.
- `marketability` — commercial/media value.
- `transferPull` — combined market signal.

## Consequences
- Real match, transfer, social and manager events can affect the player profile.
- Media stories affect fame according to credibility, heat and story stage.
- Form organically pushes fame up or down.
- Transfer interest now considers fame, reputation and marketability.
- Values are persisted in world saves (schema V5).
- CareerWorldBridge synchronizes the career player with the autonomous world.
- FPG NEWS displays the profile.

## Anti-spam
Only stories generated during the current world tick affect fame. Historical media is not repeatedly counted every day.
