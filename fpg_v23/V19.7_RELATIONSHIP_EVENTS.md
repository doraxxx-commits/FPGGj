# FPG V19.7 — Relationship Events

Interactive relationship scenes are now a first-class layer of the career.

## Flow
World context / Relationship Action -> Scene -> Player Choice -> Relationship + Career consequences -> World Event -> News/Social.

## Scene types
- Coach: role, trust, agent escalation.
- Agent: open market, listen, stay focused.
- Club: commitment, conditions, openness to transfer.
- Media: direct statement, diplomatic response, no comment.

## Persistence
Pending and resolved scenes, chosen options, serial IDs and generation cooldowns are stored in `WorldEngine.toJson()` under `relationshipEvents`.

## UI
Career -> Wydarzenia relacji. Relationship Actions can open a newly created scene automatically.
